App Features Guide
🕉️
Mantra Counter
Tap the OM button to count your mantras. Celebrate when a full mala is complete!
📿
Custom Mantra
Tap the mantra pill to choose — 10 mantras are pre-loaded, or add your own.
💬
Secret Chat
Tap the top-right corner 3 times on the counter screen, or hold the OM button — enter password to open chat.
📎
Photo & File Share
Use the 📎 button in chat to send photos, videos, or files. Max 5MB image, 25MB video.
↩
Reply & Edit
Swipe a message to reply. Long press to copy, reply, or edit your own messages.
🔐
Password Lock
4-digit password keeps the chat section private. Only you can access it.
